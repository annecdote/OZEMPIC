About OZEMPIC

Note: OZEMPIC is a temporary name used for humor and as a sarcastic pun. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ozempic® or Novo Nordisk.

OZEMPIC is an anti-fitness and body positivity app created to challenge unhealthy and obsessive attitudes toward health, fitness, body weight, and productivity.


***** Why We Made It *****

There is a noticeable number of people who struggle with disordered eating, body image issues, obsessive health behaviors, or simply feeling guilty for resting and taking time off.

We live in a world that leaves very little room for human flaws. We are constantly encouraged to improve ourselves—to be healthier, thinner, more productive, more disciplined, and more attractive.

Eventually, it can begin to feel like nothing we do is ever enough.

Even things that are supposed to improve our well-being can become another source of pressure. Exercise becomes something we feel guilty for skipping. Food becomes numbers and nutrients instead of something we can enjoy. Rest becomes something that has to be earned.

At the same time, unrealistic beauty standards can create expectations that are difficult or sometimes impossible for natural human bodies to maintain. Insecurity itself can become profitable when people are constantly given another flaw to fix and another product to buy.


Our Approach

OZEMPIC responds to this culture through humor, satire, and intentionally reversed fitness-app mechanics.

Instead of constantly telling users to:

* exercise more
* eat less
* burn more calories
* reach another goal
* optimize every part of their lives


OZEMPIC asks something different:

What if you just… took a break?
The goal isn’t to discourage healthy behavior. It’s to remind people that health does not require perfection.
You are allowed to rest.
You are allowed to enjoy food.
You are allowed to miss a workout.
And you do not need to optimize every moment of your life to be doing enough.
